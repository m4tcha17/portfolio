## Problem

Households throw away food they already have because they lose track of what's in the pantry and fridge, and don't know what to cook with the leftovers.

## Solution

Leftover is a mobile application that tracks the ingredients users already have and generates recipe ideas from them. Managing pantry and fridge items is as easy as taking a photo, and AI-assisted cooking support suggests substitutions, meal ideas, and recipe guidance based on that photo.

The app is built as an Expo-based React Native project, with a file-based app structure, reusable UI components, and a lightweight architecture centered around the mobile client and supporting data services.
