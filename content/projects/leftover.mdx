Leftover is a mobile application designed to help users reduce food waste by tracking the ingredients they already have and generating recipe ideas from those ingredients. It focuses on practical meal planning by letting users manage pantry and fridge items as easy as taking a photo and getting AI-assisted cooking support for substitutions, meal ideas, and recipe guidance based on the taken photo. The app is built as an Expo-based React Native project, with a file-based app structure, reusable UI components, and a lightweight architecture centered around the mobile client and supporting data services.
